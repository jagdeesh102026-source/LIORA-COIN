## 3. The 70:30 Purchase Allocation Arithmetic

To guarantee mathematical safety and avoid Solidity rounding errors:
- Let $Q$ be the confirmed purchase quantity in base units.
- $\text{Primary Share} = \lfloor (Q \times 70) / 100 \rfloor$
- $\text{Secondary Share} = Q - \text{Primary Share}$

### Proof of Conservation
$$\text{Primary Share} + \text{Secondary Share} = \lfloor (Q \times 70) / 100 \rfloor + \left( Q - \lfloor (Q \times 70) / 100 \rfloor \right) = Q$$
No tokens are minted or destroyed during the split, and dust cannot accumulate.

## 4. Architectural Analysis of the 70:30 Allocation Model

Management must formally select between three technical interpretations:

### Interpretation A: Purchased Tokens Split (Buyer Deduction Model)
- **Mechanism**: Buyer purchases $Q$ tokens. Contract delivers $\lfloor Q \times 70 / 100 \rfloor$ to buyer and $Q - \text{share70}$ to Treasury/LP.
- **Impact**: Buyer receives 70% of tokens paid for. May deter retail adoption unless structured as forced vesting or staking.

### Interpretation B: Proceeds Split Model (Standard Presale Crowdsale)
- **Mechanism**: Buyer pays in USDT. Buyer receives **100% of purchased OXENIQ tokens**. The received USDT is split 70:30:
  - 70% of USDT routed to Liquidity Pool reserve.
  - 30% of USDT routed to Operational Treasury.
- **Impact**: Standard Web3 presale structure. Zero penalty for buyer.

### Interpretation C: DEX Fee Sharing Model
- **Mechanism**: 70:30 applies to trading fees generated by PancakeSwap LP tokens.
